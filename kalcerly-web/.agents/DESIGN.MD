## Brand & Style

This design system targets urban runners, cyclists, and fitness enthusiasts bridging physical movement with decentralized Web3 incentive layers. The brand personality is unapologetically athletic, razor-sharp, driven, and technologically forward. It evokes high-cadence momentum, personal mastery, and cultural belonging under the rallying cry: "Make Movement a Culture".

The visual style merges modern high-performance athletic interfaces (inspired by Strava and Nike Run Club) with a refined Web3 cyber-neon aesthetic. Rather than noisy, distracting neon glow, the design operates on a disciplined foundation: deep obsidian glass surfaces, crisp geometric typography, subtle internal borders, and targeted bursts of high-voltage kinetic lime (`Volt Green`) designed to channel user attention directly into call-to-actions, telemetry stats, and AI verification badges.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for dark environments with strict visual hierarchy and high daylight contrast for outdoor athletics.

- **Primary (`#A3E635` / `#84CC16`)**: Electric Volt Green represents active kinetic energy, personal velocity, and primary conversion pathways (e.g., "Download APK", "Mulai Berlari"). Text rendered on primary surfaces must use the deep obsidian background (`#0F1115`) for AAA contrast.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4`)**: Cyber Cyan communicates AI activity validation, automated cadence tracking, and telemetry feedback.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`)**: FIT Token Amber Gold delivers reward signaling, streak badges, and on-chain balance notifications.
- **Neutrals (`#0F1115`, `#16181E`, `#1F232B`)**: Deep obsidian layers form the spatial depth. Pure black (`#000000`) is avoided on cards to retain atmospheric depth and soft rim illumination.

## Typography

The type system blends the contemporary, geometric clarity of `Plus Jakarta Sans` for narrative prose and headlines with the technical, monospaced-adjacent cadence of `Space Grotesk` for real-time telemetry, pace readouts, status pills, and FIT Token counters.

- Headers feature tight letter-spacing (`-0.02em`) to project velocity and physical authority.
- Telemetry figures and numbers utilize tabular numerals (`tnum`) to eliminate layout jitter when updating stats or mileage.
- Slogan and brand hero treatments maintain strict sentence or title casing, avoiding pseudo-futuristic all-caps degradation to preserve editorial credibility.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system operates on an 8-point harmonic spatial rhythm designed for responsiveness between mobile viewport app screens and high-impact landing page viewports.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12-column responsive fluid grid with maximum container width of `1280px`, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and `2rem` (32px) margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `1.5rem` (24px) margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) outer margins. Vertical breathing space between landing page sections scales between `space-2xl` (mobile) and `space-3xl` (desktop).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established via subtle dark glassmorphic tiers and atmospheric edge light, rejecting harsh directional shadows in favor of ambient backdrops:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: Solid `#0F1115`.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Sections)**: Background `#16181E` with an ultra-fine 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Device Frames)**: Background `rgba(31, 35, 43, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and subtle outer glow `box-shadow: 0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
- **Level 3 (Primary Focus & Kinetic CTAs)**: Background `#A3E635` paired with dynamic halo elevation `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -2px rgba(163, 230, 53, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The geometric silhouette combines aerodynamic softened curves (`roundedness: 2` / 16px default border radius for cards) with athletic pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for interactive buttons, status tags, and segment filters. 

Android device showcases use precise hardware bevel profiles (36px inner corner curvature), ensuring authentic representation of the live mobile application interface.

## Components

### Call-to-Action Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Button (Direct APK / Play Store)**: Full pill radius (`rounded-full`), height `52px` (desktop) / `48px` (mobile), background `#A3E635`, text `#0F1115` bold `15px`. Includes subtle forward motion arrow (`->`) that shifts +3px on hover. Focus rings employ an outer offset of `#A3E635`.
- **Secondary Glass Button ("Pelajari Ekosistem" / "Web3 Whitepaper")**: Full pill radius, dark obsidian background `rgba(22, 24, 30, 0.8)` with 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#F3F4F6`. Hover state shifts border to `rgba(163, 230, 53, 0.4)`.

### Metric & Activity Telemetry Cards
- Constructed with `#16181E` glass backdrop and `16px` border-radius.
- Contains an icon pill container (e.g. flame for streak, lightning for pace, diamond for tokens) paired with a high-contrast `Space Grotesk` stat counter.
- Badges feature micro-pills with colored leading dot indicators (e.g. Green dot for `AI VERIFIED`, Amber for `+45 FIT`).

### Status Badges & Pills
- Small spatial badges (`padding: 4px 10px`, radius `9999px`).
- Verification badge: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)` background with `#10B981` text and micro checkmark icon.
- Token reward badge: `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)` background with `#F59E0B` text and bold token designation.

### Input Fields & Authentication Containers
- Follow the established mobile auth patterns: Dark container `#16181E`, input background `#0F1115`, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, and focus border `#A3E635`.
- Active checkmarks utilize rounded pill toggles filled with Electric Volt Green.

### Showcase Device Framing
- Sleek modern device silhouette with `36px` corner curvature, deep slate bezel outline, and subtle ambient back-glow reflecting the screen's kinetic activity route.
